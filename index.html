Frequently Asked Questions

What is the false ceiling rate per sq.ft. in Ambernath, Thane, Badlapur & Ulhasnagar?

Plain gypsum board false ceilings start at ₹65–₹85 per sq.ft., while designer double-layer POP ceilings with LED cove lighting range between ₹95–₹140 per sq.ft. Plus-minus POP moulding starts around ₹50–₹65 per sq.ft. We offer exact measurements and transparent quotes with zero hidden charges.

How many days does it take to complete a 1BHK or 2BHK false ceiling?

A standard 1BHK or 2BHK hall and bedroom false ceiling typically takes 4 to 7 working days including GI framing, board fixing, jointing, and primer readiness.

Do you also handle electrical wiring and LED light cutting for the ceiling?

Yes, we provide precise laser cutouts for COB spotlights, panel lights, fan hooks, and cove LED strips, coordinating smoothly with your electrician or providing complete end-to-end work.
